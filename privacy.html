Alex OS Privacy Policy

Effective October 1, 2026.

Alex OS is a private personal assistant built for and used by one person, Alex Xiao. It is not available to the public.

What data it accesses

With Alex's permission, Alex OS reads and updates events in his Google Calendar, and reads his Google Health data (activity, sleep, and nutrition).

How the data is used

The data is used only to provide Alex's own assistant features, such as planning his schedule and reviewing his training. It is stored on Alex's own computer.

Sharing

The data is not sold, not shared with third parties, and not used for advertising. It is sent to an AI model provider only as needed to answer Alex's own requests.

Google API data

Alex OS's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
